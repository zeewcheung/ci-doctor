# Contributing

External contributions are not actively solicited. A submission creates no expectation that it will be reviewed, merged, or supported.

After the account holder approves a contribution policy, proposed changes should be limited to the offline classifier, synthetic fixtures, documentation, and local tests. Contributions must not include real incident logs, private code, customer data, credentials, internal URLs, copied proprietary workflows, or claims about real-world accuracy.

Every fixture must be newly synthetic and visibly labeled `FICTIONAL LOCAL DEMO` or `FICTIONAL / SYNTHETIC`. Tests must run without network access, hosted Actions, new software installation, or external credentials. A pull request, if enabled later, would grant no service entitlement and would not authorize repository scanning or customer contact.

By submitting a contribution, you agree that it may be distributed under the repository's MIT License. Do not submit material you do not have the right to license on those terms.
