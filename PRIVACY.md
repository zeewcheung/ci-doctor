# Privacy

Operational policy; not legal advice.

## Public GitHub interactions

GitHub Issues and Discussions are public repository records. Submit only non-sensitive metadata requested by the repository form. Do not submit logs, workflow contents, private code, customer or employee data, secrets, credentials, internal URLs, archives, screenshots, or attachments.

Issue metadata is retained under the repository and GitHub account settings until it is edited or deleted by an authorized maintainer. CI Doctor makes no claim that public GitHub records follow the paid-service working-file deletion schedule.

## Proposed paid-service intake

Formal materials are accepted only after a paid relationship and a separate safe-intake process have been established. Initial intake is limited to sanitized logs, a minimal reproduction, or a customer-volunteered ZIP. ZIP files require manual review and are never automatically extracted. Tokens, private keys, SSH keys, production credentials, and unredacted customer data are prohibited.

Working files are scheduled for deletion seven days after delivery, or earlier on request. Only necessary activity and financial records containing no customer data may be retained. Applicable privacy terms and the exact transfer channel must be confirmed before accepting real material.

The included artifacts are FICTIONAL LOCAL DEMO data and contain no customer material.
